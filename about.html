FIELD GUIDE / 01

A river. A plane. One more bridge.

River Run 3D is a 3D arcade flight game with procedurally generated rivers, islands, bridges, and enemy encounters. Follow the water, dodge riverbanks and obstacles, and shoot your way through each bridge. Inspired by the arcade adventures of the ZX Spectrum era, with procedural worlds and ray-marched 3D graphics.

Keep flying

Watch your fuel: fly over fuel depots to refill. Destroy targets to earn points; every 8,192 points awards an extra plane. Destroyed bridges become respawn checkpoints. Your best score is saved in this browser.

Choose the chase camera for a view along the river or switch to the top-down camera for a wider view. Press Start before each flight.

Phone & tablet

Tap Start, then hold the arrow buttons below the game to steer. Hold Speed + or Speed − to change speed; release to return to cruising speed. Tap FIRE for each shot. You can steer, change speed, and fire with several fingers at once.

Use Pause, Camera, and Restart above the touch controls. Controls release when you lift your finger, and the game pauses when you hide the page.

A different river every time

Try route 123456789 or fly the same seed with a straight river. Below the game, use Straight river to switch the river shape or Random route to generate a new seed. Either starts a new game. Share the game’s URL to let someone else fly the same route.

The game needs a browser and device with WebGPU support, served over HTTPS or localhost. Sound is off by default — use the Sound button below the game to turn it on or off
